FAQ
Common questions
How accurate are the repair cost estimates?
The estimates are based on national averages adjusted for the property's location, size, age, and condition signals in the listing. They're designed to be close enough to tell you whether a deal is worth investigating further — not to replace a contractor quote. Always get a real estimate before you buy.
Does it work on off-market or FSBO listings?
Currently HouseFlipAI works with URLs from Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com, Homes.com, Trulia, Movoto, Coldwell Banker, and Century 21. Off-market and FSBO deals aren't supported yet, but that's on our roadmap.
What if the URL doesn't work or gives an error?
Make sure you're pasting the full listing URL (not a search results page). If the listing has been removed or marked as sold, the analysis may not work. These cases don't count against your monthly limit — you'll see a clear error message and can try another URL.
Does the free plan really not need a credit card?
Correct — no credit card required to start. You get 3 free analyses every calendar month, forever. If you want unlimited analyses or deal alerts, you can upgrade anytime from your dashboard.
How is this different from just using Zillow's Zestimate?
Zillow's Zestimate tells you what a home might be worth today — it doesn't factor in repair costs, your holding costs, closing costs, or what the home could be worth after renovations. HouseFlipAI calculates all of that and gives you an actual profit estimate and a flip score so you can compare deals side by side.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes — cancel anytime from your account settings with no fees or penalties. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period, then drop back to the free plan automatically.
